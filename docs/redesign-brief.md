# The website redesign: the brief (from Stack Lead, 2026-11-08)

Bart's ask: "give you the freedom to redesign it in the best possible way. I think it could be better."
Start it the week after the Stack landing page lands (that one comes first: the App Store launch).

## The outcome
A founder who used to be an athlete lands on bartcagara.com, sees in seconds that this is for him,
believes it, and books the Restart Audit. The redesign is judged on that: visits that book a call.

## How
1. Measure first: visitors per page, how many reach "Book my call" and how many book (PostHog,
   Cal.com), over the last 90 days. That number is the bar the new page has to beat.
2. Full freedom on design, layout, structure and visuals (Claude Design is allowed).
3. Words that already book clients stay unless you can show why a change books more: the
   "last restart" angle, the headline, the FAQ answers are his proven voice (business.md,
   brand-voice.md, the post-check skill's proof of what booked him clients).
4. Everything true: no number, client result or claim the records don't back.
5. One finished page beside the current one, desktop and phone, shown to Bart in the lead chat.
   Nothing goes live until he says so.
6. Then ship it, verify it live, and keep measuring bookings for two weeks after.
