# PART 0. THE SPINE

## Four spines, one bar sentence each

- **(a) Last 24 weeks, next 24 weeks.** "Look at your last six months, then picture the next six going the other way."
- **(b) Health is the stake.** "Your body has started sending warnings, and this is how you answer them before it's too late."
- **(c) Built on the busy months.** "Every plan you tried broke on a trip or a busy month, and this one is built on them."
- **(d) Same places, the other way. (My pick.)** "You've gotten in shape before and lost it at the same places every time: a conference, a heavy month, a vacation. This time you get the body, and those are the exact places you find out you kept it."

## My pick, in five lines

1. The thing every signed man raised was the restart (13 of 13), and the doubt buyers admit is keeping it. The headline already promises that. The spine should explain the headline, not compete with it.
2. Bart's moments (the conference where he holds his weight, the shirt off on vacation, the father with energy) and the reasons every attempt died (the conference, the vacation, the month with nothing left) are **the same places**. Before, that is where it ended. After, that is where he finds out it held. One story, both halves.
3. It lets health, looks and family all onto the page without a second problem. They are not three arguments. They are three places the same result shows up: the doctor's office, the pool, the lake.
4. All the proof on file is already in this shape, in clients' own words: "Weight stayed at exactly 200", a hotel week in Chicago, "my doctor can shut up about putting me on statins", "not embarrassed to take my shirt off anymore", same weight after a vacation 9 months after we finished.
5. Bart said it himself on 3 Oct: "the ease of being around in these situations that were before setting you up for failure, right now, you have it sorted. That's the difference."

## Why the others lose

- **(a) loses as the spine and wins two places on the page.** It is a frame with nothing inside it. It worked as an email because that man had already clicked the booking link three times. Cold, it asks him to imagine, and any coach could sign "your next six months can be different." What makes Bart's email work is its last clause, "for once it doesn't fall apart when work gets crazy", and that clause is spine (d). So the frame is kept where a frame is useful: the page's second screen runs January to July (his last six months), and the last screen is the email itself, almost word for word.
- **(b) loses because health is why now, not why Bart.** A man with a heart scan on his mind still has to believe this attempt will hold, or he does another 90 days. Leading with it means either a scare line (ruled out) or talking past the 9 of 13 buyers who came for another reason. It is on the page three times, each a real thing: a client's doctor and his statins, Bart's own cholesterol card, and "it's getting tougher with age."
- **(c) loses because it is the reason, told from Bart's side.** It is correct and it is what round 6 was. Travel and logistics posts: 6 posts, 0 signups. On 3 Oct Bart rejected eight drafts that "described the program and not what the man gets." (c) is one section of this page (the news), not the story.

## The headline and subhead stay

"Get in shape one last time." is spine (d) in six words. The subhead already names who it is for and the places (trips, client dinners, a stressful month). Nothing to change. Bart has had both in front of him since round 3 and the builder has them live in the preview.
